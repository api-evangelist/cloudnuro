Gartner reports the average enterprise overspends between 20 and 35 percent on Microsoft 365 licenses due to misaligned license types and inactive users, while Forrester finds automated license reclamation alone can cut annual M365 spend by up to 18 percent for large organizations. The guide outlines 12 tactics including license inventory visibility, role-based assignments, quarterly audits, and automated reclamation, capable of delivering 15 to 25 percent savings.
